# Menzy SEO Strategy

## Positioning

Menzy is a browser-based restaurant operations product combining AI-assisted menu digitization, branded digital menus, table-specific QR ordering, a menu-grounded AI waiter, and staff order management. Product facts are sourced from `src/content/seo-pages.ts`, `src/lib/seo-schema.ts`, and the public site.

## Page architecture

- `/`: broad product/category intent.
- `/qr-code-menu`: QR menu and menu generator intent.
- `/table-ordering` and `/restaurant-ordering-system`: ordering workflow intent.
- `/ai-digital-menu` and `/restaurant-menu-maker`: menu creation and digitization intent.
- `/ai-waiter`: AI guest-assistance intent.
- `/digital-menu-for-restaurants`, `/digital-menu-for-cafes`, `/digital-menu-for-cloud-kitchens`: audience/use-case intent.
- `/restaurant-order-management`: kitchen and staff workflow intent.

## Market approach

- India: use INR pricing and restaurant/café/cloud-kitchen language already supported by the product. Avoid location pages without local proof.
- International English markets: use clear USD-compatible product language, but do not claim local tax, compliance, or payment support without evidence.
- Every page must have one primary intent, factual capabilities, a signup CTA, and contextual links to adjacent product pages.

## Authority plan

Earn links through useful restaurant operations resources, relevant software directories, integration/partner listings, and editorial outreach. Do not buy links, send unsolicited outreach, or treat a submitted form as a published backlink.
